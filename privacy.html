What we collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimisation and process only the personal data strictly necessary for lawful purposes.

Registration and account data
Mobile number, email address, date of birth, and cryptographic authentication credentials.
Profile and linkage data
Legal or preferred name, gender, dates of significance, and the cryptographic linkage code used to establish the connection between two Data Principals.
User-generated content
Photographs, audio-visual materials, textual journal entries and bucket list selections voluntarily uploaded to the shared interface.
Usage and interaction metrics
Telemetry detailing platform engagement, including logged activities, saved geographical locations and feature utilisation.
Technical data
Device identifiers, IP addresses, operating system specifications and related security diagnostics.
Geolocation data
Precise spatial coordinates collected exclusively during active use of the application, subject to your explicit and revocable consent. The Platform strictly prohibits continuous background location tracking.

Why we process it

Pursuant to Section 4 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, digital personal data is processed solely for the following specified, clear and lawful purposes:

  • Execution of core services. Registration data, linkage data and user-generated content are processed to perform the contract — specifically, to maintain the linked digital environment and synchronise real-time updates.
  • Service curation. Active geolocation data and interaction metrics are processed to furnish localised recommendations. This relies on explicit consent and does not involve invasive behavioural monitoring or the monetisation of data through third-party advertisers.
  • Security and compliance. Technical data is processed to maintain network integrity, prevent fraudulent activity and ensure compliance with statutory obligations.

Restriction on artificial intelligence training

The Data Fiduciary expressly stipulates that identifiable relationship data, including user-generated content and personal reflections, shall not be used as training data for artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Any analytics derived for platform optimisation shall be strictly anonymised and aggregated.

Governance of shared digital assets

Because the Platform links two Data Principals, the following protocols apply to changes in relationship status or account termination.

Content ownership and attribution

Each Data Principal retains proprietary rights and exclusive deletion authority over the specific user-generated content they originate. A Data Principal cannot unilaterally delete content uploaded by the connected partner.

Dissolution of linked accounts

If the linkage between two Data Principals is severed, the joint digital interface is converted into a static, read-only archive. Both Data Principals retain continuous access to previously generated historical content. No subsequent modifications or additions to the archived interface are permitted, subject to each individual’s statutory right to request erasure of their personally uploaded data.

Individual account termination

Should a Data Principal request complete erasure of their account, all individual authentication credentials and private profile data are permanently deleted. Previously shared user-generated content remains accessible within the surviving Data Principal’s interface, recognising the joint nature of the historical record.

Public dissemination and community discovery

Visibility modifications

The authority to change the privacy configuration of a shared environment from Private to Public is presently restricted to the Data Principal who initiated the original linkage.

Scope of public disclosure

Once designated as Public, only explicitly designated content — milestones, selected media and bucket list items — is rendered visible to third parties. Private textual entries, direct communications and restricted reflections remain strictly confidential.

Protection of minors

The Platform is strictly restricted to individuals who have attained the age of majority, eighteen (18) years or older. The Data Fiduciary does not knowingly collect, process or retain the digital personal data of children. Upon registration, users are legally bound to verify their age. Upon discovery of any account operated by a minor, the Data Fiduciary shall execute immediate suspension and permanent data erasure.

Data retention and erasure

In compliance with Section 8(7) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the Data Fiduciary shall not retain digital personal data beyond the period necessary to satisfy its stated lawful purpose.

Inactivity protocol

Accounts registering zero activity for a continuous period of twenty-four (24) months are designated for permanent deletion. The Data Fiduciary will issue reasonable prior written notice before executing such deletion.

Your rights

Pursuant to Sections 11 to 14 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, Data Principals hold the following rights:

Right to information
To obtain a summary of the personal data undergoing processing and of the underlying processing activities.
Right to correction and erasure
To rectify inaccurate data and to demand erasure of personal data where the purpose for processing has been fulfilled or consent has been withdrawn.
Right to withdraw consent
To unilaterally withdraw consent for optional processing activities, including location services, at any time and without terminating your account.
Right of nomination
To nominate an individual to exercise your statutory rights in the event of death or incapacity. Nomination must be affirmatively executed by you; a connected partner does not hold default nominee status without explicit designation.

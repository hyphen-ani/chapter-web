how a chapter begins

1

Both of you join

You each sign up on your own phone. One of you shares a code, the other accepts, and your two sides become a single book. Your own profile stays yours.

2

Live the week

Add a place, a film, a photo, a line about the day. Either of you can write and it lands on the same page, dated, with no feed to perform for.

3

Read it back

Months later it's the ordinary weeks you'll want. Open any month and it's all still there — where you went, what you watched, what you said about it.

What's inside

01 / 04

it writes itself, while you live

Two of you, one book. Whatever either of you adds turns up on the same page, dated, so you're never sitting down to write it later.

  • Photos, video and writing in a feed that belongs to both of you
  • Your own side, and the side you share
  • Open other couples' chapters when you're out of ideas

the list you actually get around to

Add it once and it shows up for both of you. Do it together and it stops being a plan and becomes a page.

  • A shared list that syncs the second either of you edits it
  • Ideas built for two people, not a group of six
  • Rate it afterwards, so you remember which ones were worth it

stop asking where do you want to go

Swipe places and films side by side. It only counts when you both say yes, and everywhere you actually go fills in your map.

  • Swipe together, match only on a mutual yes
  • Films and shows you can start on the same night
  • A map that fills in behind you, one date at a time

nobody reads it unless you let them

A chapter stays shut until you say otherwise. Open it to everyone, to a few people, or to nobody at all — and change your mind whenever.

  • Public, people you've added, or only the two of you
  • Messages and personal details encrypted either way
  • Leave and take your own pages with you

things people ask first

If yours isn't here, write to hello@joinchapter.app and a person will answer.

A mobile app for two people. You and your partner keep one shared book — the places you go, the things you watch, the photos you take and the small stuff in between — instead of scattering it across five apps and a camera roll.

You each sign up on your own phone, then one of you shares a code. Once it's accepted your two sides join into a single chapter, and you both keep your own profile alongside it.

Yes — a chapter takes two people to open. You can look around on your own in the meantime, and nothing you add is lost while you wait for your partner to join.

You decide, and you can change it whenever you like. A chapter can be public, visible to people you've added, or closed to everyone but the two of you. Messages and personal details are encrypted either way.

You can close a chapter without deleting it, and either of you can leave with your own pages. Nothing is held hostage, and nobody gets notified on your behalf.

Nothing to download and nothing to use during early access. Paid features will come later, and we'll tell you exactly what changes before anything does.

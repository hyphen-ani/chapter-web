frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know before writing your first chapter.

Start for free

Chapter is a shared social space designed exclusively for couples. You and your partner create a chapter together — a beautiful, growing profile that documents your relationship, adventures, and all the things that make you, you.

Each partner signs up individually and links their accounts. You each get your own side — and together you build a shared profile. Post photos, log places, track movies, and share your story with the world.

Yes — Chapter is built for two. Both partners create accounts and link them using a unique code. You can start exploring while you wait for your partner, but the full experience begins when you're both on board.

Chapter is designed for couples. However you can browse and discover other couples' chapters for inspiration. When you find someone special, you'll be ready to start yours together.

Absolutely. You control what's public and private. Individual profile details, messages, and sensitive data are fully encrypted. Your chapter profile can be set to public, friends-only, or fully private — your choice.

Chapter is free to download and use. We're in early access — sign up now and enjoy everything for free as we grow. Premium features will be introduced thoughtfully, always with couples in mind.

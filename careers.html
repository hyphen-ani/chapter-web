so we are clear about this

we don't do hustle culture.

We're building something for people's relationships. It would be strange to wear out our own to do it. Do good work, then go home to whoever is waiting.

what we're here for.

Chapter exists to help two people stay close. We hold the company to the same three things we hope the app gives back to the people who use it.

a healthy life

Sleep, food, movement, sunlight. Work fits around them, not the other way round. Nobody here is proud of a 2 a.m. commit. Laptops close at seven.

admit two

happy relationships

Partners, parents, friends, flatmates. The people you go home to matter more than any sprint, so we plan the work around them — not the other way round.

for the ones you love

good mental health

Bad weeks happen to everyone. You say so, we adjust the plan, and nobody keeps score. Asking for help is part of the job.

rest is part of the work, not a break from it.

how we look after you.

Good work comes from people who are rested, well, and close to the people they love. So that’s what we spend on.

show up for your people

A monthly relationship budget, for everyone.

Every month, we cover time with the people in your life. Use it for date night with your partner, or a Hinge subscription if you’re looking. Or dinner with the friends you keep saying you’ll meet. Or taking your parents out.

No forms and no questions about your relationship status. Just go.

Take care of yourself

  • Wellness, covered. Gym, yoga, climbing, a sports league, or whatever keeps you moving.
  • Health insurance for your whole family. You, your family and your parents, from day one.
  • Therapy, no questions asked. Fully covered, fully private.

Rest like you mean it

  • Generous leave, plus your birthday off.
  • Summer Fridays. Half days on Fridays through May and June.
  • The holidays are actually off. The whole company shuts down from Christmas to New Year.
  • A sabbatical after 4 years. Four paid weeks to do something that isn’t work.

Grow on our dime

  • A yearly learning budget. Courses, certifications, whatever makes you better.
  • Any book, any time. Just expense it.
  • Speak, ship, publish. We sponsor a conference every year and reward open-source contributions, public talks and patents.
  • Hack Week, twice a year. Some of our best features started there.

Eat well, work well

  • Stay late and dinner’s covered.
  • Getting home is on us. Late-night cabs are covered.

Set up to do your best work

  • Your gear, your way. A MacBook Pro, a monitor of your choice, and a home office setup budget.
  • The tools you’d pick yourself. Claude, Cursor and Copilot for work, plus Claude Pro for your personal use.
  • Own a piece of it. Every full-time hire gets ESOPs.

the chapters we work in.

We don't hire against a list of job titles. If your work lives in one of these, or somewhere close, we'd like to hear from you.

contents

  • i. Engineering The iOS and Android app, and the sync that keeps two phones on the same page.
  • ii. Infrastructure Servers, security and privacy — keeping private things private, and fast.
  • iii. Design Every screen, word and small detail, from the first shared code onwards.
  • iv. Marketing Telling couples we exist — honestly, warmly, and without the growth hacks.
  • v. Social media Our Instagram and everywhere couples scroll. Warm, honest, never noisy.
  • vi. Video editing Short films and reels about real couples and their ordinary Tuesdays.
  • vii. Research The Intimacy Project: how relationships begin, change and last.
  • viii. Community The first couples on Chapter, and the places worth going to together.
  • & something else? Tell us what you'd bring. We'll make room for it.
